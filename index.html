agentic RAG

ClauseCheck

ClauseCheck reads a health insurance claim and decides whether to pay it, and it shows its work. Every clause it relies on comes with the insurer, section and page. When it can't find the evidence it doesn't guess; it hands the case to a human.

The payable amount comes from a plain Python rules engine, because I wanted the same claim to give the same number every time. On a small set of labelled cases it beat a basic RAG setup on verdicts, citations and recall.

full-stack AI app · in progress

AI Job Recommender

I’m building this for Pursue Academia. You upload a resume and it pulls fresh listings from free job APIs, scores every job against your profile, explains the best matches in plain language and emails the good ones, never the same job twice.

The score is the part I care about. The backend works it out from skills, experience, education, semantic similarity and preferences, and the language model only explains the result. It can’t invent a skill or change a score. It’s a work in progress, and I’m testing it piece by piece.

  • Next.js
  • Postgres + pgvector
  • Celery
